Site Plan: PureLuxe Hair

Site Name

PureLuxe Hair — This name conveys the brand’s focus on premium, luxurious raw hair extensions. The word “Pure” highlights the 100% raw, unprocessed quality of the hair, while “Luxe” emphasizes luxury, elegance, and indulgence.

Optional Domain: pureluxehair.com (or similar)

Site Purpose

The site will serve as an elegant digital storefront for selling high-quality raw hair bundles, wigs, and extensions. It will provide detailed product descriptions, care instructions, client testimonials, and a smooth checkout experience. It also includes contact information, an about page, and a blog for hair care tips.

Scenarios

  • Where can I purchase high-grade raw hair bundles online?
  • What are the differences between raw hair and virgin hair?
  • How do I properly maintain my PureLuxe raw hair extensions?

Color Schema

The primary colors chosen reflect luxury, softness, and elegance.

#A14B56
– For headings and brand accents
#E7CBA9
– For background blocks and buttons

Typography

  • Playfair Display – Used for main headings and titles to convey luxury and sophistication.
  • Open Sans – Used for body text, product descriptions, and paragraph content for readability.
